Method

Evidence before narrative

Source hierarchy

Primary records and official documents come first. Reporting and expert analysis clarify rather than replace the underlying record.

Explicit limits

Each product separates established facts, inferences, forecasts, unresolved questions, and missing evidence.

Human direction

AI may assist with synthesis and production. Research direction, verification standards, and final editorial judgment remain human-led.
