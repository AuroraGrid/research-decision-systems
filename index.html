Method

Evidence before narrative

Source hierarchy

Primary records and official documents come first. Reporting and expert interpretation are used to clarify, not replace, the underlying record.

Explicit limits

Each product separates what is established, what is inferred, and what remains unresolved. Missing evidence is treated as a constraint.

Human direction

AI may assist with synthesis and production. Research direction, framing, verification standards, and final editorial judgment remain human-led.
