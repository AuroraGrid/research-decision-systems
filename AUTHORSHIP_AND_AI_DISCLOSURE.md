# Authorship and AI-Assistance Disclosure

Hasan Raza Kazmi directs the research questions, analytical framing, evidence standards, source selection, product architecture, editorial review, quality assurance, and publication decisions for Research & Decision Systems.

AI systems may assist with:

- code generation and debugging;
- document organization;
- drafting and rewriting;
- extraction and summarization;
- interface production;
- test generation and repetitive quality checks.

AI assistance does not replace source verification, evidence classification, analytical accountability, or final human review. Material claims should be traceable to cited records, and unsupported AI-generated content must not be published as fact.
